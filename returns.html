Returns Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our Commitment

We take pride in every tin of saffron we ship. Because our products are food items, we cannot accept returns after they have left our facility. This is standard practice for food retailers and required by food safety regulations.

But we stand behind every order.

If Something Isn't Right

Email sales@azaadsaffron.com within seven days of receiving your order and we will make it right. That includes situations like:

  • Damaged tins or broken seals
  • Wrong product shipped
  • Missing packages
  • Concerns about the saffron itself

How We Make It Right

Depending on your situation, we will ship a replacement at no cost, issue a full refund to your original payment method, or offer store credit toward a future order. We aim to respond to every email within twenty-four hours during business days.

A Note About Saffron

Saffron has a distinctive floral aroma and a slightly bitter, honey-like taste that develops over time in dishes. This is what real Grade I Super Negin tastes like. If you have questions about how to use saffron before you buy, our journal at azaadsaffron.com/blog has cooking guides, dosage information, and tips for getting the most from every thread.

Filing a Claim

To make the process smooth, please include the following in your email:

  • Your order number (from your confirmation email)
  • Photos of any damage, if applicable
  • A brief description of what happened

We do not require you to return the product before we send a replacement or issue a refund.

Contact

We would rather talk with you than have you wonder about your order. Reach us anytime at sales@azaadsaffron.com.
