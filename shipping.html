Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Where We Ship

We ship to all fifty United States, plus DC, Puerto Rico, and US territories. We do not currently ship internationally.

Processing Time

Orders are processed and shipped from Irvine, California within two business days of purchase. Orders placed on weekends or holidays begin processing on the next business day.

Shipping Method

All orders ship via USPS Ground Advantage with tracking and one hundred dollars of included insurance. Orders for our 1 ounce tin ship with signature confirmation for added protection.

Delivery Time

Most orders arrive within five to seven business days of purchase. This includes our two business day processing window plus USPS Ground Advantage transit time of three to five business days depending on destination.

Deliveries to Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories may take longer. USPS provides tracking on all shipments so you can see exactly where your package is at any time.

Shipping Costs

Free US shipping applies to our 4 gram and 1 ounce tins. Our 1 gram tin ships for $8 flat rate anywhere in the United States.

Tracking Your Order

You will receive an order confirmation immediately upon purchase. A separate email with tracking information will follow as soon as your package ships, typically within one to two business days of your order.

Lost or Delayed Packages

USPS Ground Advantage is reliable but delivery times can occasionally vary. If your tracking shows no update for more than five business days, or your package appears lost, contact us at sales@azaadsaffron.com with your order number. We will investigate and either send a replacement or issue a refund.

Damaged Shipments

Our tins are sealed and packaged for safe transit, but occasional damage does occur. If your order arrives with a damaged tin, dented package, or broken seal, contact us within seven days at sales@azaadsaffron.com with your order number and a photo of the damage. We will ship a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund.

Incorrect Shipping Address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address provided by the customer. If your order is returned to us due to an incorrect address, we can reship at your cost or issue a partial refund excluding shipping.

Contact

Questions about shipping: sales@azaadsaffron.com
